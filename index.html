Our Mission

Education with Purpose

Our mission is to provide quality education that develops students academically, morally, and spiritually. We believe every student has unique potential waiting to be discovered.

We combine a rigorous academic curriculum with Islamic values, creating well-rounded individuals ready to serve their families, community, and nation.

Our Journey

School History

1998

School Founded

Westbridge Junior & Upper School was established with 3 classrooms and 60 students.

2005

Secondary Section Added

Classes 9 and 10 introduced. First batch of Matric students graduated with distinction.

2012

New Building Inaugurated

Modern three-storey campus with science labs, computer lab, and library opened.

2025

Today

Over 850 students, 42 staff members, and a proud legacy of academic excellence.
